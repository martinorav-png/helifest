# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are Tallinn residents aged 20-40 who attend concerts, clubs, and cultural events and need to decide where to go during the festival. Secondary users are participating venues, artists, DJs, promoters, cultural organisations, collectives, and partners.

## Product Purpose

HELI makes Tallinn's independent club ecosystem legible as one two-day showcase festival, helping people discover unfamiliar venues and artists while moving easily between events.

## Positioning

HELI is a multi-venue festival whose shared pass and festival shuttle turn movement between independent locations into part of the experience.

## Operating Context

Visitors check the programme before and during two nights, compare overlapping sets, buy or retrieve a festival pass, and use shuttle stops to navigate among venues. Venue managers need structured programme, venue, and information pages that can later be edited in WordPress.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The prototype includes homepage, programme, venue detail, artist detail, tickets, transport/locations, and information views; route-aware navigation; schedule filtering; empty states; and responsive mobile layouts. Programme facts not supplied in the brief are clearly marked as illustrative. Confirmed ticket price: EUR15. Confirmed dates: 16-17 October. The final platform will be a custom WordPress theme supplied from structured entries for venues, artists, programme entries, transport routes, information pages, and site settings.

## Brand Commitments

Use the supplied geometric HELI logo, a restrained black/white/neutral-grey palette, Stack Sans Notch, large direct typography, strong alignment, and the supplied poster identity. Avoid colourful festival cliches, generic cards, decorative gradients, and motion that does not support navigation.

## Product Principles

- Programme information remains the central visitor tool.
- The route between venues is an experience, not an afterthought.
- Festival identity unifies venues without replacing their individual character.
- Dense information earns calm, exact composition.
- Visitors should always understand the next useful action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain high contrast, semantic headings, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced-motion support, touch-friendly mobile controls, and layout resilience for long names and missing imagery.
