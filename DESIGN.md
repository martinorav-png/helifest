# HELI Design System

## Visual authority

The supplied HELI identity: geometric black letterforms, Stack Sans Notch, white ground, near-black type, and precise grid relationships. The brand is typographic and structural, not decorative.

## Color and depth

Use #F7F7F5 as the page surface, #080808 as the primary foreground, #6E6E6A as a supporting neutral, and #FFFFFF only for reversed route panels. Depth is created with rules, scale, inversion, and spacing rather than shadows, gradients, glass, or rounded cards.

## Typography

Use Stack Sans Notch through the local variable font at `assets/StackSansNotch-VariableFont_wght.ttf`, with Helvetica/Arial as resilient fallbacks. Headlines are compact, bold, and deliberately large; schedule data uses tabular numerals. Body text stays legible and intentionally measured.

## Layout and components

Use a 12-column desktop and 4-column mobile grid with hairline rules. The HELI mark behaves as a navigational geometry, appearing as a map key and route token. Controls are rectangular, text-first, and clearly selected by inversion. Programme rows align time, artist, venue, and route context; they are never generic cards.

## Motion

One restrained route-line draw and a filtered-row transition are permitted. Essential information is visible without animation, and all motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
